# Steer Club: trigger, value, next step

## Trigger

A person sees a metabolic-health claim, wonders whether it applies to them, or feels blamed for something shaped by more than willpower. Their immediate question may be safety, muscle loss, weight returning, whether it is only about diabetes, or whether an approach fits them.

## Value

Steer Club offers a short, anonymous, source-linked learning round—not a diagnosis or treatment recommendation. The person makes a first-read guess, chooses the doubt on their mind, then answers seven small questions. Exactly two address that concern. Each answer unfolds a plain-language note and its sources. A personal Wrapped summarizes the game score, learning pattern, misconception and one careful answer to the chosen doubt. No weight number, account, name or phone is requested in Phases A–C.

## Next step

Play the quick quiz or myth/fact round; share a 9:16 story or public result if desired; invite one friend to the same fair seven-question order; or try a low-pressure three-day habit together. Daily puzzles, a stitched streak and a private doubts/myth tracker support return visits without a leaderboard or automatic messages. If a question is personal, speak with a clinician who knows your history. The game cannot tell you whether a treatment is suitable or safe.

## Guardrails

The landing curve is an illustration, not measured data. Health claims remain qualified and source-linked. No drug names, dosing, efficacy promises, invented testimonials/statistics, or claims of clinician review are used. Anonymous server records are stored for game/challenge continuity; browser storage clearing does not delete those server records. Self-serve server deletion, care/lead capture and clinician-reviewed care copy remain Phase D work.
