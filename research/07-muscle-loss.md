# Weight loss and muscle loss

**Claim:** “Weight loss is only fat loss.”

**Verdict:** MYTH

Weight reduction can include both fat and lean tissue, including muscle. A 2017 peer-reviewed review concludes diet-induced weight loss reduces muscle mass and that adequate (not excessive) protein and physical activity, particularly resistance exercise, can help preserve it [1]. A 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis found resistance exercise during dietary weight loss protected fat-free mass and increased fat-mass loss versus diet alone, though certainty differed by outcome and duration [2].

**Card copy (23 words):** Weight loss can reduce fat and lean tissue—not just fat. Resistance exercise and enough protein may help preserve muscle, but results vary.

**Caveat:** Lean or fat-free mass is not identical to skeletal muscle. Evidence and effects vary by person and intervention; protein needs differ, so this is educational information, not personal medical advice.
