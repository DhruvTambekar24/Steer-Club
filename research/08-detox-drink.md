# Detox drinks reset your metabolism

**Verdict: MYTH** — the claim that detox drinks reset metabolism is unsupported. This does not mean diet has no effect on health or metabolism: the narrower point is that no specific drink has been shown to reset metabolism or detoxify the body.

**Card copy (21 words):** “What you eat can affect your health, but no specific drink has been shown to reset metabolism or detoxify your body.”

The British Dietetic Association says there are no specific drinks that can do a better or “magic” detox job; it describes the body’s organs as continually processing and removing waste. [1] The U.S. NIH’s NCCIH reports no compelling research supporting detox diets to remove toxins and notes that juice-only programs may produce initial weight loss through low calorie intake. [2] A peer-reviewed critical review found very little clinical evidence for detox diets and no randomized controlled trials of commercial detox diets in humans at the time of review. [3] These sources address detox diets/programs generally; they do not establish that every dietary exposure has no metabolic effects.

**Caveat:** Diet can affect health and metabolism, and evidence on commercial detox programs is limited. This educational copy is not personal medical advice.
