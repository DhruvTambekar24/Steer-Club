# Claim check: “Metabolic problems only affect people with diabetes.”

**Verdict: MYTH.** Cardiometabolic concerns are not exclusive to diabetes. Prediabetes describes blood glucose above normal but below the diabetes diagnostic threshold; insulin resistance and other cardiometabolic risk factors can also be present before a diabetes diagnosis. This finding does not imply any particular person has a condition.

**Card copy (21 words):** No. Prediabetes and other cardiometabolic risk factors can occur before diabetes is diagnosed, and some occur in people without diabetes.

**Caveat:** “Metabolic problems” is broad and can refer to different conditions or risk factors. This is general education, not an assessment or diagnosis; individual health status cannot be inferred from this copy.

## Sources

1. **NIDDK, “Insulin Resistance & Prediabetes.”** Defines prediabetes as blood glucose above normal but not high enough for type 2 diabetes; describes insulin resistance and conditions associated with it.
   https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance

2. **Brannick et al., “Prediabetes and Cardiovascular Disease: Pathophysiology and Interventions.”** Peer-reviewed review reports that cardiovascular risk starts increasing before clinical diabetes and discusses cardiovascular risk factors among people with prediabetes.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5806140/

3. **Anjana et al., “Metabolic non-communicable disease health report of India: the ICMR-INDIAB national cross-sectional study (ICMR-INDIAB-17).”** India-wide study reports prediabetes prevalence of 15.3% and diabetes prevalence of 11.4%, establishing that prediabetes is common in India and distinct from diabetes; it also measures hypertension and dyslipidaemia as metabolic NCDs.
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37301218/
