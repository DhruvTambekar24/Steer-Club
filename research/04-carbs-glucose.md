# Evidence note: sweets and blood glucose

**Claim assessed:** “Only sweets raise blood sugar.”

**Verdict:** MYTH.

Sugars are not the only carbohydrate that can raise blood glucose. The CDC states that sugars and starches raise blood sugar, and lists grains and potatoes among starchy foods. The ADA explains that carbohydrate-containing foods are broken down into glucose, and names rice, whole-wheat bread and starchy vegetables as carbohydrate foods. NHS guidance independently identifies rice, potatoes and bread as starchy foods and part of a balanced diet. These sources support correcting the claim without implying that such foods must be avoided.

**Card copy (25 words):** Sweets aren’t the only foods that can raise blood sugar. Starchy foods like rice, roti, potatoes and grains can raise it too. Foods differ; no need to avoid them all.

**Caveat:** Blood glucose responses vary by person, portion, preparation and the rest of the meal. Educational information only; not personal medical advice.
