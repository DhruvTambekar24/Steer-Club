# Evidence note: “Weight is mostly willpower”

**Verdict: MYTH** — The claim wrongly makes willpower the main explanation. WHO describes obesity as arising from complex interactions among environmental, social, biological and behavioural determinants, and notes biological susceptibility and metabolic pathways. NHLBI likewise says risk is shaped by more than eating amount, including activity, sleep, behaviors, health conditions, genetics and the environment. An Endotext review also describes social and environmental contributors such as food access and socioeconomic conditions. The evidence supports rejecting the claim without suggesting that any one factor determines a person's weight. [1] [2] [3]

**Mobile card copy (26 words):** Body weight is not simply a matter of willpower. Biology, habits, social conditions and surroundings all play a part, and no single factor explains everyone's weight.

**Caveat:** Educational information, not personal medical advice; individual experiences vary.
