## Phase B — Game, personalized readout and Wrapped — complete locally
- Provide two game modes with seven items each, selected/shuffled from a larger editable bank of 30+ items at `/content/questions.json`, seeded by date so every user sees the same daily puzzle on the same day. Quick quiz is three-option multiple choice on food, movement, sleep and body. Myth-vs-fact supports swipe or tap with drag physics; swipe left = myth, right = fact; show “myth” stamp in maroon and “fact” in rust.
- Every answer reveals a sliding explanation sheet of no more than about 35 words in plain English with a source tag. Show correct-answer combo as a rising stack of paper, stitched-thread progress, and light haptic or soft paper sound, muted by default with a corner toggle. Include conservative Indian-context topics: post-meal walking; refined carbs/pairing with dal, vegetables or curd; metabolism and willpower; sleep and appetite; muscle protection; thali/meal choices; South Asian risk at lower body weight/around waist; ghee/oil; fasting claims; detox drinks; juice vs fruit; sitting; tea timing; vegetarian protein; safety; regain; and diabetes-only misconceptions. Never name drugs. Every item has a `sources` array drawn from WHO, ICMR, ADA or peer-reviewed reviews; every claim is defensible, otherwise soften or remove it.
- Use the pre-game selected doubt to choose exactly two of the seven questions that address it. End the Wrapped with a “what matters most for someone like you” card that answers the selected doubt honestly.
- Create a 9:16 result story with auto-advancing tap-through slides, full-bleed maroon and cream and large italic serif numerals: “You cracked X of 7 myths”; archetype/emblem (Myth Slayer 7, Myth Buster 5–6, Fact Finder 3–4, Curious Beginner 0–2); topic strengths as stitched bars for food/movement/sleep/body; the myth believed hardest with one-line correction; one takeaway personalised to weakest topic; hero guess vs reality (e.g. “You guessed 80%”); and the personalized doubt-answer card. Share to WhatsApp, download 1080×1920 PNG for Instagram Stories, or copy link. Public `/r/[id]` gets a dynamic OG image and beautiful WhatsApp preview. No weight numbers. ## Phase D — Care, warm lead, analytics, admin, privacy — implemented locally; integration pending
- `/care` asks “What’s on your mind?” with the five doubt chips (Is it safe? Will I lose muscle? Will the weight come back? Is this only for diabetics? Is it even for me?). Tapping each opens an honest answer and is recorded. Safety needs clinician review and isn’t for everyone; nausea can happen; muscle can be lost with any weight loss and protein/strength work help; weight may return when support stops so habits/follow-up matter; metabolic health is not only diabetes; eligibility is a doctor’s call.
- Explain support options: habit coaching, clinician review, and clinician-guided therapies where appropriate. GLP-1-based treatment may be mentioned as a category only; never name specific drugs or brands, dosing, efficacy numbers or promises. No fake clinician-review claim or testimonials.
- Provide a three-step warm lead form: first name, validated 10-digit Indian mobile (+91), preferred time, language and explicit consent checkbox. Show exactly what the doctor’s team sees as “the note we pass on”: score, weakest topic, doubts tapped (including the selected personalized doubt), streak and UTM source. POST `/api/lead` stores and forwards to the supplied webhook/CRM/Google Sheet URL, which must be provided before external forwarding. Confirmation gives only a supplied/verified callback window; never invent one. Use DPDP-aware explicit contact consent, minimal collection and deletion request path. Provide privacy data-storage notice, sources/review page, clear educational-not-medical-advice and talk-to-a-doctor line, and urgent-symptom emergency note; never diagnose.
- English plus Hinglish microcopy toggle; architecture ready for Hindi and Marathi. Track `hero_view`, `dial_locked`, `mode_selected`, `question_answered` (correct/time), `game_completed`, `wrapped_viewed`, `share_clicked` (channel), `challenge_created`, `challenge_joined`, `challenge_completed`, `care_opened`, `doubt_tapped`, `lead_started`, `lead_submitted`, scroll depth and `utm_source`. `/admin` has env-password protection and funnel/drop-off dashboard.
